How the mint works

1

Connect a wallet

Use any BNB Chain (BSC) wallet. You only need a small amount of BNB: $0.10 per apple plus network gas, which you pay yourself.

2

Pay $0.10 in BNB

The price is set in USD and converted at the live Chainlink BNB/USD rate at the moment you mint, so you always pay $0.10 worth of BNB, never a fixed amount that drifts. Overpay by accident and the contract refunds the difference in the same transaction.

3

Proceeds move on-chain

Your payment is forwarded to the project treasury in the same transaction. The contract itself never holds funds, and there is no withdrawal path for mint proceeds. The full amount collected goes to the future token's liquidity pool.

  • Public mint hands out token ids in order and automatically skips the 33 reserved Legendary ids.
  • Max 5 apples per wallet on this website. The contract itself allows up to 50 per transaction and has no per-wallet cap, so the 5 limit is enforced by this website only.
  • Artwork and metadata are pinned on IPFS, independent of this website.

FAQ

What is Apple Art?

A 3,333-piece NFT collection of soft origami-paper apple mascots on BNB Chain, with four tiers: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. One artwork per tier, assigned to token ids by a deterministic, published shuffle.

How much does minting cost?

$0.10 per NFT, paid in BNB. The contract reads the live Chainlink BNB/USD rate when you mint, so the BNB amount always equals $0.10 at that moment. You also pay the network gas for your own transaction.

Where does the mint money go?

100% of it is forwarded by the contract to the project treasury wallet in the same transaction, and 100% of it will be used for the future Apple Art token's liquidity. The contract holds nothing and mint proceeds cannot be withdrawn for anything else.

What is the team reserve?

33 of the 99 Legendary pieces are reserved on-chain for the team. The public mint skips those ids automatically, so the public can mint 3,300 pieces, including the other 66 Legendaries. The reserved ids are published in the repo.

When do the token and the share phase launch?

Both are planned for after the mint sells through. Dates and details will be announced in the GitHub repo and project channels before anything launches. Holding an Apple Art NFT is how you take part.

What do I need to mint?

A wallet that supports BNB Chain (for example MetaMask or the Binance Wallet), a little BNB for the $0.10 mint price plus gas, and this page once the mint is open.
